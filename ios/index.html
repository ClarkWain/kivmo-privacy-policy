Kivmo Privacy Policy — iOS

Effective November 1, 2026. This policy applies to Kivmo for iPhone and iPad. Contact: clarkwain@outlook.com.

Kivmo is an offline focus timer and task manager. It does not require an account and does not automatically send your tasks, focus history or diagnostics to the developer. The iOS app contains no advertising, analytics, remote-push or developer-operated cloud synchronization service.

Information on your device

Your tasks, notes, lists, tags, subtasks, dates, reminders, repeat rules, focus records, optional ratings and review notes are stored in the app’s local files. Settings and timer-recovery state are also saved locally. Statistics, goals, streaks and growth are calculated from these records.

Optional device features

Local notifications provide timer and task reminders when you allow them. Reminder text can contain the task titles you entered. You control notification previews in iOS Settings. Live Activities and widgets provide timer information or shortcuts on your device.

Flip-phone mode uses accelerometer readings to check orientation. Readings are processed in memory; Kivmo does not store a motion history or upload sensor data. Ordinary tasks and timing do not require motion access.

Background sounds and spoken reminders use bundled audio and iOS speech services. Speech reminders contain timer phrases, not task notes or audio recordings. Voice downloads and operating-system services follow Apple’s settings and practices. The app does not request microphone, camera, contacts or location access.

Files and sharing

Backups, CSV files and report images are created only when you choose to export or share them. They can contain task titles, notes and focus data. Backup files are not encrypted by Kivmo. Protect exported copies and review their contents before sharing.

You choose a destination in the system file picker or share sheet. iCloud Drive, other document providers and receiving apps handle files under their own policies. Kivmo does not automatically send exported files to the developer.

Storage and deletion

App data remains locally until you delete it. Some deletions keep underlying entries for record consistency. Removing the app deletes its current private local files; offloading an app may retain its documents. Exported files, shared copies and device backups must be managed separately.

Kivmo does not provide cloud sync. Device backup and migration services, including iCloud device backup, depend on your system settings. The developer has no server copy and cannot remotely retrieve or delete your local database.

External links and support

Opening a website or sending support email uses external services, which may process visit or message information under their own policies. A support message includes only what you choose to send; your database is not attached automatically. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information or private databases.

Your choices and updates

Manage notifications and available permissions in iOS Settings. Optional permissions affect only their related features. This is a general productivity app without child-specific accounts or advertising. We update this policy when app data practices change.

Support

For help with Kivmo, local data, or this policy, email clarkwain@outlook.com. Include your app version and a description of the issue, without unnecessary personal information.
